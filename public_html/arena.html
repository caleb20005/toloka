METHODOLOGY

A reliability score,
not a demo score.

Toloka Arena tests agents in controlled, realistic environments with live tools, changing context, and business rules. We repeat equivalent tasks to capture whether a system is consistently useful, not just occasionally correct.

01
Fresh environmentsTasks and data are held out from model training and public benchmark contamination.
02
End-to-end successScoring considers correct tool use, policy adherence, and a completed task state.
03
Repeated trialsEvery score aggregates independent task attempts to surface reliability and variance.
